Life Insurance- Life insurance is a contract between the insurer and the policyholder. Under this plan, the insurer promises to provide life cover in exchange for regularly paid premiums. This means that in case of the policyholder's untimely death during the policy term, the insurer will pay the selected death benefit (as per applicable terms and conditions). Apart from the death benefits, several life insurance plans also provide wealth creation and tax-saving benefits. Life insurance includes term life insurance plan, whole life policy , endowment plans , NRI investment plans , money back plans, ULIPs , child Plans , investment plans and retirement plans. 2. General Insurance– Also known as non-life insurance, general insurance is defined as any insurance that that doesn’t fall in the category of life insurance. As per applicable terms and conditions, it provides pre-decided insurance coverage to the insured in exchange of a specific premium. General insurance includes car insurance, health insurance , two-wheeler insurance, travel insurance, home insurance, corporate insurance, critical illness, and personal accident insurance.
